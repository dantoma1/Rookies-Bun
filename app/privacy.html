Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 3 June 2026

Rookies is a platform that connects early-career talent in the Netherlands with employers. We take your privacy seriously and process your personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Dutch implementing law (UAVG). This policy explains what data we collect, why, who has access, and the rights you have.

1. Who we are

Rookies is operated by the founding team behind Rookies, based in Tilburg, the Netherlands. A legal entity (Rookies B.V.) is in the process of being registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK); we will update this policy with the registration number and registered office once that completes. For privacy questions, please email privacy@rookies.nl.

The data controller is Rookies. Where we use third-party services to handle data on our behalf (for example, our database provider), those parties act as processors or sub-processors under written agreements.

2. What data we collect

If you sign up as a student

  • Identity and contact: full name, email address, phone number (optional), alternate email (optional).
  • Academic information: university, programme, degree level, year of study, expected graduation date, GPA (optional), field of study, current status.
  • Profile content: CV or résumé (uploaded file), avatar image (optional), bio, project descriptions, courses, skills, languages, organisations and extracurriculars, interests, stated career goals, preferred roles, locations, employment type.
  • Links: LinkedIn URL (optional), portfolio URL (optional).
  • Activity: jobs viewed, jobs applied to, messages exchanged with employers, file attachments shared in those messages.
  • Notification preferences and account settings.
  • Technical: IP address (for session security), browser and device information, login timestamps.

If you sign up as an employer or recruiter

  • Company name, sector, size, description, website.
  • Contact person and additional recruiters on the hiring team: name, role, email, phone (optional).
  • Content you create: job postings, application reviews, shortlists, saved-candidate lists, messages and any file attachments you send in conversations.
  • Verification status of the company account (pending, approved, rejected).
  • Technical data as above.

If you join the waitlist for a Rookies Cycle

  • The account you signed in with (student or employer), your name, and the cycle identifier you joined.
  • Timestamp of joining. We use this only to notify you when the cycle opens.

If you send us a message through the contact form

  • Name, email address, and the content of your message. We use this only to reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of the conversation.

3. Why we collect it and on what legal basis

We process your data on the following lawful bases under GDPR Article 6:

  • Consent — when you create an account and choose to make profile content visible to other users.
  • Performance of a contract — to deliver the platform service you signed up for: matching, applications, messaging.
  • Legitimate interest — for fraud prevention, platform security, and improving how the matching algorithm works.
  • Legal obligation — where applicable (e.g. responding to lawful requests).

We use your data to:

  • Match you with relevant jobs (if you are a student) or relevant candidates (if you are an employer), including through Rookies Cycles. Some of this involves automated processing — see Section 5.
  • Display your profile to verified counterparties when you apply, when an employer's job is shown to a student, or when a cycle reveal makes you part of a match.
  • Notify you when the cycle you joined the waitlist for opens.
  • Send transactional emails about your account, applications, and messages.
  • Improve platform features.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use your data to train external AI models.

4. Who has access to your data

Internal access

Members of the Rookies team — currently a small founding team — access user data only as needed to operate, support, and improve the platform.

Processors and sub-processors

We use the following third parties to handle data on our behalf. Each operates under a written Data Processing Agreement.

  • Supabase Inc. / Supabase BV — database, authentication, file storage, and serverless functions. Your data is stored in the EU-West-1 region (Dublin, Ireland). Supabase's privacy terms: supabase.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic, PBC — provider of AI inference used in automated processing on the platform (see Section 5). Anthropic is based in the United States. Data transfers are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Anthropic does not retain prompts or responses for training. Anthropic's privacy terms: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting, CDN, and cookieless web analytics. Cloudflare's privacy terms: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
  • Email delivery: transactional emails (account confirmations, password resets, cycle notifications) are sent via Supabase's built-in email service in the EU region. If we add a dedicated transactional email provider in the future, we will update this list before activating it.

Other users

Parts of your profile become visible to specific other users when you take an action — for example, when a student applies to a job, the employer who posted that job sees the student's profile. We tell you clearly before any such action what the other party will see.

5. Automated processing

Parts of the platform use automated processing — including AI inference — to help surface relevant matches between candidates and roles. The specific signals, models and weights are part of our proprietary matching technology.

No part of this processing makes legally significant decisions about you on its own. Every interview invitation, offer and acceptance is made by a human (the candidate or the employer), so this processing does not fall under the prohibition in GDPR Article 22(1).

You have the right to object to automated processing. To opt out, email privacy@rookies.nl and we will exclude your account from it.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Active accounts: as long as your account remains active.
  • Inactive accounts: deleted automatically after 24 months of inactivity.
  • After a deletion request: removed from active systems within 30 days; encrypted backups purged within a further 60 days.
  • Application records: retained for the duration of the application process plus 6 months.
  • Cycle records (matches, shortlists, fit scores): retained for 12 months after the cycle closes, then anonymised.
  • Waitlist entries: deleted within 30 days after the cycle they refer to has opened (or after you ask us to remove you).
  • Contact-form messages: retained for 24 months from the date of the message, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • Message attachments and uploaded CVs/avatars: retained for as long as the related conversation or profile exists; deleted when the parent record is deleted.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics: retained indefinitely (these cannot be used to identify you).

7. Where your data is stored

Primary storage is on Supabase infrastructure in the EU-West-1 region (Dublin, Ireland). AI fit explanations involve a transient data transfer to Anthropic in the United States, governed by Standard Contractual Clauses. No other personal data leaves the EU.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and associated data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data in specific circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest, including profiling.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • Complain — to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens): autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

The fastest way to exercise the right to erasure is the "Delete my account" button in your profile settings. For all other requests, email privacy@rookies.nl. We respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies — primarily an authentication cookie that keeps you logged in, and basic security cookies. We do not use marketing cookies, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics with personal identifiers. For this reason, we do not display a cookie consent banner.

If we later add non-essential cookies or trackers, we will show a clear consent banner and update this policy before they are set.

10. Security

We protect your data through:

  • Encrypted transport (HTTPS on every page).
  • Encrypted storage at the database layer (Supabase-managed encryption at rest).
  • Row-level security policies on every table containing personal data.
  • Limited administrative access on a need-to-know basis.
  • Regular security reviews and dependency updates.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach occurs and is likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the Dutch Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and inform affected users without undue delay, as required by GDPR Articles 33 and 34.

11. Children

Rookies is intended for users aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a minor under 16 has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date at the top. For material changes (for example, adding a new processor or a new processing purpose), we will email registered users at least 14 days before the change takes effect.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints:

  • Email: privacy@rookies.nl
  • Postal: postal address will be added once Rookies B.V. is registered with the KvK.
